## Included changes

- A single persisted Home timeline for historical totals, trend, spending and
  activity; current balances and forecasts remain explicitly separate.
- Native TradingView-inspired chart styling, without a WebView or new library.
- Reports Explore analytics and shared contextual charts across accounts,
  budgets, goals, loans, planned payments, transactions/search, balance and wealth.
- Root navigation remains five-tab navigation, without the legacy two-tab swipe.
- Hidden balances suppress chart geometry and monetary readouts. Financial
  ledger formulas, application identity and backup schema are unchanged.
