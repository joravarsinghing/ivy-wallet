## Changes included

- Consistent planning-screen Back controls, primary actions, neutral cards, typography and meaningful empty-state icons across Budgets, Goals and Loans.
- Clearer budget remaining/over-limit information and proportional meters; preserved category/overall limits with an explanation where they overlap.
- One contextual first-goal action; labeled loan filters instead of a second bottom navigation bar. Existing saved loan-list position is retained.
- Bounded net-worth dialogs with persistent Close/Save controls, accessible whole-row review choices, preserved review drafts and a return to review after editing.
- Grouped net-worth completion/allocation information, plain-language status copy and on-demand repayment explanations.
- Dot-free balance trend with subtle balance-only shading, distinct series line patterns, truthful gaps and expandable exact values. Plain/shaded alternatives were rendered for comparison; shaded is the default.
- Correct proportional spending-category bars and hidden-balance protection for chart geometry, scales and percentages.
- Consistent Home-card gutters and adaptive phone, square-fold, large-text, landscape and DeX candidate renders. Screenshot goldens were not replaced.
- Updated [design language](../design/JVY-DESIGN-LANGUAGE.md) and [mobile standards](../guidelines/Mobile-UI-Standards.md), informed by the requested UI/UX Pro Max and Android adaptive skills.

No database migration, financial calculation, backup/import format, application identity or signing-key change was introduced.
