# Jvy Wallet Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 17 October 2026  
**App:** Jvy Wallet  
**Maintainer:** Joravar Singh

Jvy Wallet is a local-first Android money manager. This policy explains what the app processes, where it is stored, and what happens when you choose to import, export, share, or contact us.

## The short version

- Your wallet ledger is designed to stay on your device.
- Jvy Wallet does not require an account, connect to your bank, or provide automatic bank synchronization.
- We do not sell your financial data or use it to build an advertising profile.
- Imports, exports, and backups happen when you choose them. Backup files can contain sensitive financial information.
- The app may send technical crash diagnostics when crash reporting is enabled in the installed build.

## Information stored in the app

The app can store information you enter or import, including:

- accounts, balances, currencies, and account settings;
- income, expenses, transfers, categories, notes, and transaction dates;
- budgets, goals, reserved money, planned payments, and loans;
- preferences such as theme, notifications, privacy mode, and app-lock settings; and
- records imported from supported CSV, JSON, or ZIP backup files.

This information is used to calculate balances, reports, forecasts, goals, and other wallet features. The core ledger does not require a Jvy Wallet cloud account.

## Storage and network use

Wallet data is stored locally in the app on your Android device. Jvy Wallet does not upload your ledger to a Jvy Wallet server.

The app can use the network for optional technical or supporting functions, such as crash diagnostics, exchange-rate functionality where enabled, and opening links that you deliberately select. Network access is not required to enter or review the core ledger while offline.

Jvy Wallet does not use location to create an advertising profile or to calculate your balances. Some Android permissions may be present for inherited platform functionality; the core wallet does not require location access.

## Imports, exports, and backups

When you select a file, Jvy Wallet reads it on the device to import supported data. When you export a CSV or backup, the app writes the file to a location you choose through Android’s file picker.

Exported files and backups are not cloud storage provided by Jvy Wallet and are not automatically encrypted by the app. They may contain your complete financial history. Protect them with Android device security or encrypted storage, and delete copies you no longer need. A copy shared with another app or person is governed by that recipient’s practices.

## Biometrics and privacy mode

If enabled, the app-lock feature uses Android’s device credential or biometric authentication, including fingerprint where supported. Jvy Wallet does not receive or store your fingerprint or biometric template; Android performs the authentication.

Privacy mode hides financial values in the interface. It is a display feature, not encryption. Someone with access to your unlocked device or exported backup files may still be able to access the underlying data.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, Jvy Wallet can schedule local Android notifications to remind you to record or review transactions. These notifications are generated on the device. You can disable them in the app or Android settings.

## Crash diagnostics

The current Android build includes Firebase Crashlytics support. When crash reporting is enabled, a crash report may contain technical information such as the app version, Android version, device model, stack trace, crash time, and an app-instance identifier. This information is used to diagnose stability problems, not to sell or advertise against your financial history.

Crashlytics is provided by Google and is subject to [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Jvy Wallet does not intentionally place transaction amounts, account names, or backup contents in crash reports. If a future release changes diagnostics or adds another service, this policy will be updated.

## Sharing and external links

Jvy Wallet does not share your wallet ledger with the maintainer by default. You choose when to share an export, backup, report, or link using Android or another installed app.

The app may link to external services such as GitHub, LinkedIn, and the maintainer’s website. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. Opening a link does not give Jvy Wallet access to your account on that service.

## Retention and deletion

Your wallet data remains on the device until you delete it in the app, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app, subject to Android behavior and any copies you exported elsewhere.

Deleting data from Jvy Wallet does not delete backups, CSV files, screenshots, or copies already shared outside the app. Those must be deleted from their separate locations. Technical crash reports may be retained by the service provider according to its policies.

## Children

Jvy Wallet is a general-purpose money-management tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s data practices change or when clarification is needed. The effective date at the top will identify the current version. Material changes will be communicated in the app or repository release notes where appropriate.
